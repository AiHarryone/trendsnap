Is the Halloween invite maker really free?

Yes — all 30 templates and every canvas size are free, with no watermark and no account. Ten templates are animated and export as GIF.

Are my party details uploaded anywhere?

No. Every letter is drawn onto a canvas inside your browser. Nothing you type ever leaves your device.

What is a Boo-basket tag?

In the Halloween neighbourhood game, a secret "Boo" leaves a small treat basket on a porch with a tag. The receiver makes two baskets and passes them on. Pick Boo-basket tag above to print those tags.
